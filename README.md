# Hello! I'm Diego 👋

I'm a **Computer Science student and junior backend developer** based in Dublin, Ireland.

Before transitioning into software development, I spent **15+ years in the Brazilian
payments and banking industry**, working across card processing, payment gateways,
financial integrations, and Central Bank regulations. I collaborated daily with
engineering teams, solution architects, and product managers in highly regulated,
high-availability environments.

Today, I'm building solid technical foundations while applying that domain expertise
to real projects. I'm particularly interested in **backend systems, APIs, and
distributed architectures**.
